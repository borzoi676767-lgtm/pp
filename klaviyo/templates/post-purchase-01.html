Getting the most out of the bag

01 · Rest it
Give the beans 3–5 days off the roast before you brew. It sounds fussy. It isn't — the flavour genuinely settles.
02 · Store it
Airtight, room temperature, out of the light. Not the freezer, not the fridge — condensation is the enemy.
03 · Measure it
Start at 1:16 — 60g of coffee per litre of water. Adjust from there once you know how you like it.
