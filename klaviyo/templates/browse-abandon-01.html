Twenty-odd roasts is a lot to sort through, {{ first_name|default:"friend" }}. Here's the shortcut we'd give you standing in the shop.

If you take it black → Colombia · Highland Mist
Dried orange, berry, chocolate. Bright and well-mannered — nothing to hide behind.
If you add milk → Six Summits · Espresso
A dark six-bean house blend with enough body to cut straight through oat milk.
If you drink it iced → Cold Creek · Cold Brew
Chocolate and toffee, ground coarse. Built for a long, low-acid steep.
If you need it strong → Peak Surge · Max Caffeine
Tanzanian arabica cut heavy with robusta. For 4 a.m. alarms and summit pushes.
